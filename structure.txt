Features
- Add, view, edit, and delete subscriptions.
- Record subscription name, provider, cost, and billing cycle.
- Track next payment dates.
- Identify subscriptions due within the next seven days.
- Calculate monthly and yearly subscription costs.
- Track active and cancelled subscriptions.
- Search subscriptions by name or provider.
- Generate spending reports.
